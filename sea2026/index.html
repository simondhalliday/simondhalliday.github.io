Three guiding principles

1

Spend tokens on planning. Plan with your strongest affordable model; review, then implement with cheaper models.

2

Expand your question; always audit the data. Attempt worthwhile projects you could not previously manage; decide how to check the evidence.

3

Maintain the method in files and authority with people. Save instructions for recurring work; humans (i.e., you and me) approve consequential decisions (we are the bottlenecks).

Three lessons from experience

1

Find the method through trial and error. Try a small task, inspect what failed, and revise the instructions before scaling up.

2

Budget for the whole project. Budget for review and rework; use ordinary scripts for repeatable steps.

3

Keep learning as the models change. Retest known tasks when models or tools change; yesterday’s workaround may no longer help.
